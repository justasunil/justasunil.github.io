Research

My research is centered on power and performance optimization of modern computing systems. I investigate how processor resources can be managed dynamically to improve performance per watt while respecting system-level power constraints.

During my Ph.D. at IIIT-Delhi, I worked on runtime-level power management for multicore and multiprocessor systems running co-scheduled workloads. My work combines hardware performance monitoring, workload characterization, processor frequency control, and power capping.

Research interests

High-Performance Computing (HPC), CPU/GPU performance optimization, power management, DVFS, uncore frequency scaling, power capping, runtime systems, PMU/PMC profiling, workload characterization, performance/watt, and resource management.

Research focus

I study workloads with different compute, memory, and I/O characteristics and analyze how core frequency, uncore frequency, power budgets, and application parallelism affect performance and energy efficiency.

My recent work explores dynamic power redistribution among co-running applications, frequency sensitivity, throughput optimization, and fairness under a global power budget. The resulting systems include Cuttlefish, KarmaPM, and Fulcrum.
